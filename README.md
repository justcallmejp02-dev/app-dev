# My Favorite Movie: The 7 Sundays

The 7 Sundays is a Filipino family drama-comedy that revolves around the importance of family and spending time with loved ones. The movie follows a father who asks his children to spend seven Sundays with him after learning he is terminally ill. It’s a heartwarming story about family, forgiveness, and cherishing every moment together.

## Why I Love It

The film resonates with me because it reminds me of the value of family time and reconciliation. The performances are great, and it captures the emotional struggles of balancing life, work, and relationships.
